Authoring tool layout — modern shell options v2

Further than before — leaning into current visual language: glass/blur, glow, bento grids, floating docks, dark canvases. Same steps, same data, four different feels.

1 · Glass canvas, dark boldest

Dark gradient-mesh background, a floating frosted-glass step navigator pill, and glass cards (blurred, translucent) for each step's content. Selected tiles glow cyan instead of just filling solid. The most "premium product" feel of the four — closest to what Linear/Vercel/Raycast style tools look like today.

4 · Floating dock, app-like

Almost no top chrome — a big confident title, tiles right below it, full-bleed white canvas.
