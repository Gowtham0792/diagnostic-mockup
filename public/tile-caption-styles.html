Simpler this round, all guaranteed one line: no chips, dots, numbers or stacked subtitle. B–E give the caption a wider box (no wrap) and vary only weight, color and spacing.

A · Current width why it looks cramped

110px caption box — long labels wrap to two lines and centre-break mid-word, which reads worse than "bland."

B · Same style, single line

Identical weight/color to today — just given enough width to never wrap. Confirms the box width was doing most of the damage.

C · Brand-colored label

Same plain single-line text, recolored to ZF blue instead of flat black — ties every caption back to the app's accent color, no extra chrome.

D · Refined slate, tight tracking

Slightly smaller, a dark slate instead of pure black, tightened letter-spacing, medium weight — a quieter, more "designed" typographic feel.

E · First word accented

Still one plain line — only the first word is bold ZF-blue, the rest regular-weight grey. Draws the eye without adding any shape or background.
