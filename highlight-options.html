Step indicator & selection highlight — options

Two things to make clearer: (1) which authoring step you're on, (2) which trailer tile is selected. Samples are interactive — click a tile.

A · Step indicator

Shown as a bar under the header. Steps: Select vehicle · Axle configuration · ABS layout · Parameters · Publish. Current = step 1.

S1 · Numbered pills + connectors now live

Number in a circle, label beside it, thin connectors. Current step filled blue with a halo; done steps get a check. Familiar, label always readable.

S2 · Title + progress bar + counter

Big current-step title, "Step 1 of 5", a thin fill bar. Most compact; only the current step is named.

S3 · Underline tabs

All steps as tab labels; the current one is blue with an underline. Reads like navigation; good if steps can be revisited.

S4 · Filled progress track

Like S1 but the connector segments behind completed steps fill blue — a clearer sense of "how far along".

S5 · Minimal dots

Just dots (current one elongates) + the current step name. Smallest footprint; loses the other step names.

B · Selected-tile highlight

The current border-tint is easy to miss. Options, strongest first.

H2 · Solid fill + inverted glyph + check badge + scale proposed

Unmissable: the tile becomes solid ZF blue, the trailer turns white, a check badge pops on the corner, slight scale-up. Now applied in the tool.

H4 · Elevated card + accent bar

Tile lifts with a strong shadow and gains a blue underline bar. Keeps the glyph as-is; feels tactile.

H5 · Filled header strip

A blue strip with a check slides down over the top of the tile; glyph nudges down. Distinct without inverting the whole tile.

H3 · Thick border + check badge

Conservative: 3px blue border, faint blue wash, corner check. Lowest visual disruption.

H6 · Ring pulse

Border tint + a slow pulsing ring + badge. Draws the eye; can feel busy if several selections happen in a flow.

H1 · Border tint + ring original

Light blue border, faint wash, soft ring. Subtle — the reason we're here.

Recommendation: step indicator S1 (or S4 for a stronger progress feel); selected tile H2. Both are applied in the tool now — open it — swap to any other on request.
